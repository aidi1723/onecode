# Hexagram runtime closeout, 2026-10-18

This phase closed the task-rule gaps found while running OneCode against the existing 64 hexagrams. No new hexagram was added. Version stays 0.8.0. Nothing was pushed.

The running rule is one reading:

- Pure yang and excess yang cool, except outer fire, which halts.
- Pure yin discovers.
- Excess yin keeps the five-element action in the transition itself. Earth controlling water throttles. Earth generating metal accelerates. A same-element excess yin still activates. There is no second schedule that disagrees with the transition.
- Fire, a missing executor, and water preserving a resume seed keep their halt or checkpoint.
- A tagged write, patch, command, read, or list follows that action. Reads are allowed during discovery and yang rest.
- Sibling steps are judged separately. One hexagram, or several copies of it, stays on the trace. Different hexagrams combine by the I Ching aggregate. Entropy stays on the trace and does not replace the code.
- A refusal reason classifies back to the hexagram family that produced it. A halted task exits nonzero.

`bash scripts/verify.sh` is the closeout gate for this phase.
